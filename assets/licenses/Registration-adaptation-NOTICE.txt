Open Volleyball French registration handwriting adaptation, 2026

The application's source code remains MIT licensed. The additional trained model
adaptation is made available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Retain this notice and the supplied
dataset attributions when redistributing it. The base Microsoft TrOCR model retains
its MIT license, supplied in TrOCR-MIT.txt.

Additional real handwriting used for this adaptation:

CREMMA WIKIPEDIA — Alix Chagué, Thibault Clérice, Elsa Van Kote, Jennifer Carrow
and the contributors listed in CREMMA-Wikipedia-CITATION.cff.
https://github.com/HTR-United/cremma-wikipedia
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7782065
CC BY 4.0. License text: CREMMA-Wikipedia-CC-BY-4.0.txt.
Changes: handwritten lines cropped from ALTO annotations, editorial deletion
markup excluded, contrast/cropping adapted to registration fields, used to
fine-tune TrOCR-small. No original document images are bundled.

GNHK: A Dataset for English Handwriting in the Wild (ICDAR 2021)
Alex W. C. Lee, Jonathan Chung and Marco Lee.
https://github.com/GoodNotes/GNHK-dataset
CC BY 4.0. License text: GNHK-CC-BY-4.0.txt.
Changes: selected handwritten words cropped using their polygons, EXIF orientation
applied, other regions masked, contrast/cropping adapted to registration fields,
used to fine-tune TrOCR-small. No original document images are bundled.

Supplementary synthetic short fields were created for this project. Windows font
files are not redistributed. Model recipes, dataset revisions, checksums and
evaluation limits are documented in assets/models/registration-model-card.md.

RIMES was evaluated during development but excluded from the shipped adaptation:
the original publisher's noncommercial license conflicts with a mirror's MIT tag.
No RIMES-adapted checkpoint is shipped.
